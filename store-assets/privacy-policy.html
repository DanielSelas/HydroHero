Privacy Policy for Hydro Hero

Last updated: 5 September 2026

Hydro Hero ("the app") is developed by Daniel Sela ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what choices you have.

Contact: danielsela96@gmail.com

The short version

Hydro Hero does not ask you to create an account, and it does not upload your hydration data anywhere. Your water log, goal, streak, coins and purchases are stored only on your device.

The app does use three Google services that collect limited technical data: Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, and Google AdMob.

What stays on your device

The following never leaves your phone. We cannot see it, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app or use Settings → Reset progress:

  • Your daily water goal and the drinks you log
  • Your daily history (up to the last 365 days)
  • Your streak, coins, owned shop items and selected character
  • Your reminders and notification preferences
  • Your theme choice

Settings → Export data creates a CSV of your water log and hands it to Android's share sheet. Nothing is uploaded — you choose where it goes, and if you choose nothing, it goes nowhere.

What is collected

Firebase Analytics (Google)

Anonymous usage data used to understand how the app is used — for example when a drink is logged, when a daily goal is completed, and when the subscription screen is opened. This includes an app instance identifier, approximate (country-level) location derived from IP address, device model, operating system version and language. It does not include your name, email address or your hydration data.

Firebase Crashlytics (Google)

If the app crashes, a crash report is sent so the bug can be fixed. It contains the stack trace, device model, operating system version, and diagnostic values such as your current coin balance and streak length, which help reproduce the problem.

Google AdMob

The app shows advertising to users who have not purchased Premium. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and related device and usage data to serve and measure ads. Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are shown a consent message before personalised ads are used.

Google Play Billing

Premium purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see or receive your payment details. The app only receives confirmation from Google Play of whether you own a Premium product.

What we do NOT collect

Hydro Hero does not collect, request or access:

  • Health or fitness data from Google Fit, Health Connect or any other source
  • Precise location
  • Contacts, calendar, photos, camera, microphone or files
  • Your name, email address, phone number or any account credentials

The app requests only three Android permissions: internet access, network state, and permission to post notifications for your reminders.

Sharing

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with anyone other than the Google services listed above, acting as our service providers.

Children

Hydro Hero is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your choices

  • Ads: buying Premium removes advertising. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Analytics and crash reporting: uninstalling the app stops all collection.
  • Notifications: can be turned off at any time in the app or in Android settings.
  • Your data: Settings → Reset progress erases everything stored on your device.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to, or deletion of, data held about you. Because we do not operate servers and hold no account for you, such requests concern the Google services above; contact us at danielsela96@gmail.com and we will help where we can.
